Common questions

How do I move to a new phone?

Sign in to the same iCloud account on the new iPhone and install Kept. Your data syncs automatically.

I subscribed but Pro isn't showing.

Open Kept → Settings → Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

How do I cancel?

iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Kept, or Kept → Settings → Manage subscription. Cancel during the free month and you won't be charged.

How do I add or hide a widget?

Kept → Settings → Add a widget shows how to add one. Settings → Widgets and privacy explains how to hide the count on your Lock Screen.

How do I delete everything?

Kept → Settings → Delete all data. This removes your data from your iPhone and your iCloud.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
