About Kept

What is Kept?

A private day counter for adults stepping away from a habit, such as adult content. It shows how long you've kept going, down to the second, and gives you a Panic button for the moments when an urge hits.

Who is Kept for?

Adults aged 18 and over. On iOS 26 and later, Kept asks iOS whether you're 18 or older. You can decline the prompt, and Kept never sees your birth date.

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone, running iOS 18 or later. There's no iPad, Mac or Android version yet.

Is Kept medical treatment?

No. Kept is a habit tracker. If you're struggling, please reach out to a professional. You can find a helpline near you at findahelpline.com.

Privacy

Who can see my data?

Only you. Kept has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and I don't run a server. Your data stays on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud storage, which I can't access.

Will notifications or widgets give me away?

No. Notifications only say "Check-in" or "Milestone" and how long you've kept going, for example "8 hours in. Keep going." Widgets show a day count and a small siren icon, never what the app is for. You can hide the count on your Lock Screen: Kept → Settings → Widgets and privacy.

Can I lock the app?

Yes. Turn on Kept → Settings → App lock to require Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. Kept also shows a blank screen in the app switcher.

How do I delete everything?

Kept → Settings → Delete all data. It removes your streak, slips, urges and notes from your iPhone and your iCloud, and stops all check-ins. It can't be undone.

Panic and your streak

What happens when I press Panic?

A ten-minute timer starts, because urges usually pass within ten minutes. You'll see short lines in the tone you chose, your own reasons, and a "Breathe with me" guide. You can leave after one minute, but try to stay until the ring closes.

How are days counted?

A day is a full 24 hours from your start, so the day count always matches the live timer, whatever your time zone.

What happens if I slip?

Tap "I slipped", choose what triggered it, and start again. Your current streak restarts, but your longest run and your total clean days stay. A slip doesn't erase the work you've done.

When do check-ins arrive?

Every 4 hours on the first day of a streak, then every 8 hours, plus milestones at 6, 12 and 18 hours and every day after. Nothing is sent between 11 pm and 7 am in your local time. Turn them on or off in Kept → Settings → Check-ins.

Kept Pro and subscriptions

What's free, and what's in Kept Pro?

Always free: Panic, your streak and stats, breathing, milestones, check-ins, app lock, iCloud sync and delete-all. Kept Pro adds Insights, Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, your own Panic lines and reasons, and accent colors.

How much does Kept Pro cost?

In the US: $2.99 a month, $3.99 every 3 months, or $9.99 a year. Prices in other countries are set by the App Store in your local currency. Every plan starts with a free month.

How does the free month work?

You won't be charged during the first month. After that, the plan you chose renews automatically until you cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the free month ends and you won't be charged at all.

How do I cancel?

iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Kept, or Kept → Settings → Manage subscription. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for.

I subscribed, but Pro isn't showing.

Open Kept → Settings → Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does Kept show ads?

No, and it never will.

Your data and new phones

How do I move to a new iPhone?

Sign in to the same Apple Account on the new iPhone, with iCloud turned on for Kept, and install Kept. On first launch it checks iCloud for a few seconds, then your streak, slips and Insights come back by themselves. Restoring an iPhone backup or using Quick Start also brings Kept's data over.

Do my settings sync between devices?

Your streak, reasons, tone, custom lines and accent sync through iCloud. App lock and check-ins are set separately on each iPhone, on purpose.

Does Kept work offline?

Yes. Everything works without a connection. iCloud catches up the next time you're online.
