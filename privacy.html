Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 October 2026

Kept is a private day counter for adults who are breaking a habit. It is made by Harsha Dodderi, an individual developer based in India ("I", "me"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Kept.

The short version
  • I don't collect your data. Kept has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party code, and I don't run a server.
  • What you enter stays on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud storage, which I can't access.
  • Kept is for adults 18 and over.
  • You can delete everything at any time in Kept → Settings → Delete all data.

1. What stays on your device (and in your iCloud)

Kept stores the following only on your device:

  • Your start date, the dates and times of slips and urges, and the triggers and custom reasons you log
  • The reasons you choose or write ("What do you want back?", "What does a slip cost you?") and any custom Panic lines (Pro)
  • Your settings: tone, accent color, app lock on/off, check-ins on/off

If you're signed in to iCloud, this data is synced through your private iCloud database so it can appear on your other devices. Apple stores it on your behalf under Apple's privacy policy. I have no access to it. To stop syncing, turn off iCloud for Kept in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud.

Widgets. To show your day count, Kept saves your streak start time and whether you have Kept Pro in an area of your device shared only with Kept's own widget. Widgets never show your reasons, notes or triggers.

Check-in notifications. If you turn them on, they're scheduled on your device by iOS. They contain only "Check-in" and how long you've kept going, for example "8 hours in. Keep going." They never mention what the app is for.

App lock. Face ID, Touch ID and your passcode are handled entirely by iOS. Kept only learns whether unlocking succeeded and never receives biometric data.

Age check. On iOS 26 and later, Kept asks iOS whether you are 18 or older (Apple's Declared Age Range feature). iOS shows its own prompt. Kept receives only an age range, never your birth date, and stores just the result ("adult", "under 18" or "unknown") on your device. It is never sent to me.

2. What I may receive

  • Emails you send me. If you contact support, I receive your email address and whatever you write. I use it only to reply and keep it no longer than 12 months.
  • Crash reports, only if you allow it. If you've turned on "Share With App Developers" in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, Apple may share anonymized crash and performance reports with me. They contain technical information, not your Kept data. You can turn this off at any time.
  • Sales reports from Apple. Apple gives me aggregated sales and download numbers. They don't identify you.

I never receive the content you enter in Kept.

3. Purchases

Kept Pro is an auto-renewing subscription sold through Apple's App Store, monthly, every 3 months or yearly, with a free first month. Apple handles payment, renewal, cancellation and refunds, and I never receive your payment details. Kept checks your subscription with Apple on your device to unlock Pro features. Trial reminders inside the app are decided on your device from the date Kept was first opened.

4. What I don't do

I don't sell or share your personal information, show ads, track you across apps or websites, build profiles about you, or use your data to train AI.

5. Security

Your data is protected by iOS's built-in encryption and, in iCloud, by Apple's security. You can turn on Kept's app lock, which hides the app's content in the app switcher and asks for Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings → Delete all data removes everything from your device and, through sync, from your iCloud. Check-in notifications are cancelled and widgets cleared.
  • iCloud: iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Kept.
  • Uninstalling Kept removes the data on that device. Data in iCloud remains until you delete it as above.
  • Support emails: deleted within 12 months, or sooner on request.

7. Adults only

Kept is for people aged 18 and over and isn't directed at children. On iOS 26 and later, users identified by iOS as under 18 can't use the app. If you believe a child has sent me personal information, contact me and I'll delete it.

8. Your rights

Because I don't hold your Kept data, most requests are handled directly in the app (see section 6). For anything I do hold, such as support emails, you can contact me to access, correct or delete it, or to object to how it's used. I'll respond within 30 days.

European Union / United Kingdom (GDPR / UK GDPR). I'm the controller for support emails and opt-in crash reports. I process them on the basis of my legitimate interest in providing support and fixing problems. You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and you may complain to your local data protection authority.

California (CCPA/CPRA, CalOPPA). I don't sell or share personal information and don't use it for cross-context advertising. Kept doesn't track you, so browser "Do Not Track" signals have nothing to change. You won't be treated differently for exercising your privacy rights.

India (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023). For grievances about your personal data, contact me at the email below. I'll acknowledge it promptly and aim to resolve it within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

Canada (PIPEDA and provincial laws, including Quebec Law 25). I'm responsible for privacy for Kept and can be reached at the email below. You may also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or your provincial regulator.

9. International transfers

Support emails are stored with my email provider (Gmail), which may store them outside your country under its own safeguards.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update the effective date above and, for significant changes, mention it in the app's release notes.
